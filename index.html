Privacy Policy — Beneath the Last Road

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how Beneath the Last Road handles information. Privacy and support contact: papazanstudios@gmail.com.

1. Offline gameplay and local saves

The game works offline and does not require an online account. It stores your chosen hero name, appearance, difficulty, progress, inventory, equipment, discoveries, and generated world state on your device so you can continue your adventure. Your hero name can be fictional; you do not need to enter your real name. The game does not send these saves to the developer or operate a cloud-save server.

2. Advertising, tracking, and permissions

The game does not include advertising, an analytics SDK, or a developer-operated crash-reporting service. It does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone. The current Android release does not request the Internet permission for gameplay. It does not sell your personal information or share your local saves with advertisers.

3. Google Play and device services

Google Play handles store purchases, downloads, updates, and its own platform services under Google's policies. The game does not receive your payment-card details. Google may make store reviews, sales information, and aggregate installation or diagnostic reports available through its developer services. Android or your device provider may offer backup or transfer features subject to your device settings and their own policies; these are separate from the game's local-save functionality.

4. Contacting support

If you email papazanstudios@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your question or investigate the issue you report. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. Support messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask for deletion by emailing the same address.

5. Storage, security, and deletion

Save files are stored in the application's local data directory. The game does not add encryption to these files; access is governed by your device and operating system. On Android, you can remove local game data through Settings, Apps, Beneath the Last Road, Storage, Clear storage or Clear data. Uninstalling normally removes the app's local data, but separately retained device backups may restore it. Manage or delete such backups through your device or backup provider. Starting a new run replaces the previous run when the new run is saved. There is no online game account to delete.

6. Children and personal information

The game does not ask players for a real name, age, contact details, or an online account. It has no player-to-player messaging. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to support can contact papazanstudios@gmail.com to request its deletion. The game's audience and content rating are stated separately in its store listing.

7. Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. The revised policy will show its updated date and be made available in the game and on the published policy page. For questions about privacy or deletion of information supplied to support, email papazanstudios@gmail.com.
